# wkalyana.github.io
Egypt's Digital Museum.

### History Comes Alive in 3D

Egypt Digital Museum is an immersive digital museum experience designed to bring ancient Egyptian history and artifacts to life through interactive 3D visualization, cinematic media, virtual exhibitions, and an interactive historical timeline.

The website combines modern web technology with the visual language of ancient Egypt to create an engaging way for visitors to explore historical artifacts and locations digitally.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🏺 Interactive 3D Studio
Explore ancient Egyptian objects through an interactive 3D viewer. Users can interact with and rotate different models in a 360° environment.

Featured models include:

- Mask of Tutankhamun
- Great Pyramid of Khufu
- Sacred Ankh
- Obelisk

The project uses Three.js to create the interactive 3D experience. 

### 🗿 Living Artifacts
Browse a collection of ancient Egyptian artifacts presented through interactive cards containing historical information, dates, locations, and 3D inspection options.

### ⏳ Journey Through Time
Explore Egyptian civilization through a chronological timeline featuring important historical periods and events, including the unification of Egypt, the Great Pyramid of Giza, and the Battle of Kadesh.

### 🏛️ Virtual Exhibitions
Discover themed digital exhibitions such as:

- Secrets of the Golden Tomb
- Building the Pyramids
- Guardian of the Necropolis

Each exhibition combines imagery, descriptions, and immersive digital presentation.

### 🎥 Living 3D Gallery
Experience ancient Egyptian monuments and artifacts through cinematic 3D videos, including:

- Tutankhamun
- Abu Simbel
- Ramesses II

### 📰 Museum Updates
A dedicated updates section presents new digital scans, interactive reconstructions, discoveries, and upcoming virtual museum events.

### 📩 Digital Expedition Newsletter
Visitors can subscribe to receive updates about new 3D scans, virtual galleries, and archaeological discoveries.

### 🎨 Immersive Visual Design
The interface features:

- Egyptian-inspired gold and turquoise accents
- Glassmorphism UI
- Cinematic backgrounds
- Animated transitions
- Custom cursor effects
- Responsive layouts
- Interactive hover effects
- Smooth scrolling

### 📱 Responsive Design
The website adapts to different screen sizes, including desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
