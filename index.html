Chronological Architecture

Journey Through Time

Five thousand years of civilization — from the first pharaoh to the last hieroglyph.

3100 BCE
Unification of Egypt

King Menes unites Upper and Lower Egypt, establishing the First Dynasty and founding the ancient Egyptian civilization along the Nile Valley — the birth of a 3,000-year empire.

𓋾
𓇳
2560 BCE
Great Pyramid of Giza

Pharaoh Khufu completes the Great Pyramid — one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World. Nearly 2.3 million stone blocks, some weighing up to 80 tons, assembled with astonishing precision.

1274 BCE
Battle of Kadesh

Ramesses II leads Egyptian forces against the Hittites at Kadesh, resulting in the world's earliest recorded peace treaty — inscribed in both hieroglyphs and cuneiform on silver tablets.

⚔️
𓁿
1323 BCE
Tutankhamun's Reign

The boy pharaoh Tutankhamun restored traditional Egyptian religion. His intact tomb, discovered by Howard Carter in 1922, revolutionized Egyptology and captured the world's imagination.

332 BCE
Alexander the Great

Alexander conquers Egypt, founding Alexandria. The Ptolemaic dynasty begins, blending Greek and Egyptian culture — creating a new era of art, science, and philosophical inquiry.

🏛️
𓆄
30 BCE
End of Ancient Egypt

Cleopatra VII, the last pharaoh, dies. Egypt becomes a Roman province, ending 3,000 years of pharaonic rule — but the legacy of Egyptian civilization endures for all of eternity.
